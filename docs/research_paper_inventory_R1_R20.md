* **Paper ID:** R1
* **Full Title:** A Paradigm for Rice Yield Prediction in Tamil Nadu
* **Authors & Year:** Prof. A. K. Mariappan and J. Austin Ben Das (2017)
* **Venue & Citation:** 2017 IEEE International Conference on Technological Innovations in ICT For Agriculture and Rural Development (TIAR 2017), IEEE, ISBN: 978-1-5090-4437-5/17, pp. 18–21.
* **Research Problem:** [Explicit] Developing a decision-support heuristic for predicting rice yield across agricultural seasons in the Vellar river basin of Tamil Nadu (p. 18, 20).
* **Study Location & Crop:** [Explicit] Vellar river basin, Tamil Nadu, India; Crop: Rice (Oryza sativa) (p. 20).
* **Dataset & Sources:** [Explicit] 10-year historical agricultural records from the Vellar river basin. [Inferred] Extremely small sample: only 11 training rows are listed in Table II (p. 20), and 1 single test query row in Table III (p. 20).
* **Remote Sensing / Satellite Imagery:** [Explicit] None used. Zero remote sensing or satellite observations.
* **Variables Used:** [Explicit] Soil type (Mixed, Loamy), Fertilizer application (Nitrogen, Potash, Phosphorus in kg/ha), Crop duration (90, 120 days), Nitrogen concentration (ppm), Season (Rabi) (p. 20, Table II). Climate variables are absent.
* **Algorithms:** [Explicit] ZeroR classifier algorithm (paired probability mean heuristic) (p. 19, 21).
* **Model Architecture & Prediction:** [Explicit] Non-neural, tabular probability pairing. Outputs expected mean yield for matched soil/fertilizer bins (p. 20–21).
* **Validation Strategy & Evaluation Metrics:** [Explicit] Evaluated on 1 query instance. Metric: Mean Absolute Error (MAE) (p. 21).
* **Reported Results:** [Explicit] Predicted training mean: 4.8363 t/ha; reported Mean Absolute Value = 0.0364 (p. 21).
* **Main Claim:** [Explicit] Provides an early paradigm showing that rice yield in Tamil Nadu river basins can be estimated from agricultural input parameters (p. 21).
* **Limitations Acknowledged:** [Explicit] Authors note the lack of prior yield prediction systems in Tamil Nadu and state that additional environmental factors should be considered (p. 18, 21).
* **Future Work Suggested:** [Explicit] Integrating automated meteorological data and expanding beyond the Vellar basin (p. 21).
* **Gaps Relevant to Our Project:** Sample size is microscopic (11 rows); no time series or climate data; no satellite integration; relies on an obsolete ZeroR heuristic.

* **Future Work Suggested:** [Explicit] Expanding across all districts of Punjab and incorporating SAR imagery to bypass cloud cover (p. 560).
